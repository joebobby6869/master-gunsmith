About the simulation

Accept a commission, purchase material lots, and work through design, timber selection, stock shaping, decorative metalwork, fitting review, finish, and client inspection. Each stage has costs, time, skill checks, and potential rework. This is a business and craftsmanship game, not an instruction guide for manufacturing functional firearms.

All prices, wages, time requirements, and skill probabilities are illustrative game balancing figures—not documented historical price quotations. Save data stays in this browser via localStorage. No accounts, downloads, or external dependencies are required.
